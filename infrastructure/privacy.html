Keep faces and plates protected during routine patrol and sharing.

Control disclosure

Record access

Log every view, reveal, and export. Patrol operators cannot edit the tamper-evident audit trail.

Protect the originals

Restrict access to unredacted recordings and apply defined retention limits.
