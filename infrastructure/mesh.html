UNIFIED COMMAND VIEW · tactixglobalMCT

View all connected assets in one command-center system. Collect timestamped events, observations, and video clips from multiple vehicles and fixed sensor units over the mesh.

GENERATE END-OF-DAY REPORTS

tactixglobalMCT uses an LLM to process events collected across units and compile an end-of-day mission report for operator review.

SEARCH EVENTS BY VOICE

Ask tactixglobalMCT to find events by timestamp or time range—for example, “Show events from Vehicle 2 between 14:00 and 15:00.”

ELEVATE SENSING AND CONNECTIVITY

A drone tethered to Vehicle 1 provides an elevated camera and a relay path above ground-level obstructions. Maximum operating height: 50 meters.

INTEGRATE FIXED SENSOR TOWERS

Pole-mounted nodes combine a top-mounted PTZ, acoustic sensors, mesh radios, and enclosed edge compute and battery power, with a side-mounted solar panel. They coordinate sensing and share intelligence with the vehicles.

CONNECT WITH EXISTING MESH

Integrate with compatible mesh infrastructure to extend intelligence sharing to another vehicle or command center.

OPERATE INDEPENDENTLY

Local processing and mesh communication without external Wi-Fi, cellular service, or cloud dependency.

SHARE WITH MISSION CONTROL

Share selected observations, video clips, and intelligence across the local mesh without relying on external Wi-Fi or cellular networks.
